afterward — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

afterward is a personal reflection and activity-logging app. It lets you record things you have done and thoughts you want to keep. Your entries are intended to remain on your device.

This policy explains what information afterward stores, how it is handled, and what happens when you choose to export or share it.

1. Information you enter

afterward may store the following information that you choose to enter:

  • Entries: activity descriptions, dates, times, notes and associated emotion or mood selections.
  • Ideas and notes: optional titles, note content, and creation and modification timestamps.
  • App preferences: settings you choose within the app, such as appearance preferences.

2. Where your information is stored

afterward stores entries and notes locally on your device using its local storage.

In this release:

  • afterward does not require you to create an account.
  • afterward does not provide cloud storage or account-based synchronisation.
  • Your entries and notes are not routinely transmitted to an afterward-operated server.
  • afterward does not use advertising identifiers or behavioural advertising.
  • afterward does not include analytics or identifiable crash-reporting services.

Your information may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its data, or lose or reset your device. We recommend keeping your own copies of any information you want to preserve.

3. Exporting and sharing

afterward provides export functionality so you can create copies of your information.

When you choose to export or share information, afterward prepares the relevant file and invokes your device's sharing interface. You decide whether to share it and which available app or recipient receives it.

Once you share a file with another app or person, that recipient's privacy practices and handling of the file apply. afterward does not control copies shared outside the app.

4. Permissions

The Android release does not request access to sensitive device features such as your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Android may use application-level permissions internally for platform functionality. These do not, by themselves, give afterward access to your personal information.

5. Children's privacy

afterward is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through an account or online collection service.

6. Data security and your control

Your entries and notes are stored locally on your device. You are responsible for securing access to your device and maintaining backups of information you wish to keep.

You can manage your entries and notes within the app, including deleting individual items or using the available delete-all-data function.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available at the policy URL published in the app's Google Play listing.
